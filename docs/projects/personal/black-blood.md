## Role
Solo Developer

Fast. Stylish. Unforgiving.

## ABOUT
Merge the gunplay of DOOM with the flashy combos of Devil May Cry.
Survive intense combat arenas by constantly switching weapons and
mixing melee/ranged attacks.

## FEATURES
✓ Seamless gun + melee combat

✓ Dynamic style ranking (D/C/B/A/S tiers)

✓ Parkour movement (dash, wall-run, swing)

✓ Multiple melee and ranged weapons

and much more..
